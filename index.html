Overview

About the company

Rohan Engineering Works is an industrial job-work and component partner based in Bhosari, Pune. We help manufacturers, machine builders and automobile-component customers get machining, fabrication, cutting, tooling and supply work done through one point of contact. Every job starts with understanding the drawing, material, quantity, tolerance and delivery need — so scope, price and timeline are clear before work begins.

Where a job needs capacity beyond our own, we coordinate with suitable vendors, follow up on quality and deliver as agreed.

Vision

To be the most trusted single-window industrial partner for precision job work in the Pune industrial belt.

Mission

Deliver drawing-accurate work on the agreed date, with clear communication and honest feasibility at every step.

      FAQ

      Frequently asked questions

      Can I send a drawing for quotation?

      Yes. Include material, quantity, critical dimensions, finish and required delivery date. PDF, DXF, STEP or clear photos/sketches can be shared on WhatsApp or email.

      Do you own all listed machines?

      This page does not claim every process is performed in-house. Some work may be coordinated through external vendors after feasibility and terms are confirmed.

      How long does a quotation take?

      It depends on drawing complexity and vendor response. A complete enquiry can be reviewed faster; we confirm the expected time when you contact us.

      Is there a minimum order quantity?

      Single prototypes and repeat batches can both be enquired. Pricing and feasibility vary by job and are confirmed in the quotation.

      Can you supply inspection reports or certificates?

      Mention inspection and certificate needs in your enquiry. Availability depends on the job and source and is confirmed before order acceptance.

      Will my drawing stay confidential?

      Mention any confidentiality requirement in your enquiry so it can be agreed in writing before sharing sensitive drawings.

      Does the form save data online?

      No. This single HTML file prepares WhatsApp/email messages and can save drafts locally. Online storage needs a hosted backend and database.
